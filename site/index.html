Community

Profile and Group Tab Creator

Add your own tabs and sub-tabs to member profiles and groups, choose who sees them, and tidy up the built-in BuddyBoss tabs.

  • Custom profile tabs
  • Custom group tabs
  • Sub-tabs
  • Who sees each tab
  • Rename built-in tabs
  • Hide built-in tabs
  • Drag to reorder
  • Your own content
  • Shortcodes
  • Bulk actions & drafts

Upgrading from the old BuddyBoss Tab Creator? Your tabs, rules and built-in tab changes are copied over once, and the old data is left untouched.

Up and running in a minute.

1

Download

Get buddy-modules.zip. The button always gives the newest version.

2

Upload & activate

In wp-admin, go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, then activate.

3

Turn on modules

Open Buddy Modules in the admin menu and switch on what you need.

Requires WordPress {{WP}}+, PHP {{PHP}}+ and BuddyBoss Platform 2.8+.
